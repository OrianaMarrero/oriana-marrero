About me

I am Oriana Marrero, an interior design student at Sheridan College with a background in Chemical Engineering and a passion for creating meaningful spaces.

Originally from Venezuela and now based in Canada, I bring a unique mix of creativity, technical understanding, and cultural perspective to every project.

My designs focus on emotional connection, storytelling, and spatial functionality—whether through colour, texture, or flow. With experience in event design, visual arts, and administrative roles, I adapt easily, communicate clearly, and thrive in collaborative environments.
